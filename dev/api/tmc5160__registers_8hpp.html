Detailed Description

TMC5160 register map: addresses, access rules, reserved bits and reset values.

Copyright
Copyright (c) 2026 The driver-tmc5160 authors. SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT

Datasheet chapter 6 (Rev. 1.18), pages 31 to 56. Addresses are seven bits. Most registers are write-only (the datasheet marks them W, section 1.3 page 7 asks the master to keep a shadow), so the table below records, for every address, whether the chip lets the driver read it, whether the driver may write it, the bits that exist (every other bit is written as 0) and the bits that clear on a write of 1. The driver refuses a read of a write-only address and a write of a read-only one.
